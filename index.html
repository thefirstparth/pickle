Executive summary

Q.1Am I playing harder now?
Yes — 34% more effort per minute since January. Time above 80% of max heart rate rose from 5% of play to 39%. The trend has not reverted.
Q.2Am I playing more?
Less often than the Jul–Aug 2025 peak, but each session burns far more. May 2026 nearly matched Aug 2025's calories in eight fewer hours.
Q.3Is it making me healthier?
In-game fitness, unambiguously. Recovery markers, not yet — sleep is absorbing the gains. Section 08 carries the finding and the recommendation.

§ 08

What the Body Says

The honest audit. The game got harder; the recovery markers did not follow. Resting heart rate drifted from 58 to 66 bpm across the year and HRV is flat. The data points at one suspect — and it is not the sport.

FIG. 8.1
Resting heart rate
Monthly average, bpm · lower is better
RHR improved to 57.6 during the Jul–Aug 2025 volume peak, drifted up through the layoff, and has held in the mid-60s despite harder play. Volume helped it; intensity alone has not.
FIG. 8.2
Heart rate variability
Monthly average SDNN, ms · higher is better
HRV peaked at 50 ms in the same Jul–Aug 2025 block and has chopped between 39 and 47 since — no upward trend despite a fitter heart in-game. That points at recovery, not fitness.
FIG. 8.3
Cardio fitness (VO₂max)
All 47 readings since 2023, ml/kg/min · Apple estimates this only on outdoor walks and runs
The all-time best is 40.7 from Jul 2023, in the running era. Within the pickleball era the 38.0 of Aug 2025 is the high-water mark. Current readings sit near 34 — but the instrument only updates on walks and runs, which barely happen now. The true ceiling is likely higher than charted.

Finding
The sessions themselves are clean: next-day recovery is identical to rest days and resting HR is marginally better after playing. The leak is sleep. The 8–11 pm games cost about thirty minutes each against a 5.5-hour baseline that has no buffer to give. The 2026 intensity is earned fitness, but it will only surface in HRV and resting HR once the nights stop paying for the evenings. The Jul–Aug 2025 block proves the upside is real — the one period where volume, RHR, HRV and VO₂max all moved together. Recommendation: same gear, earlier games, in bed by 11:30.
