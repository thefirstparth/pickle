Am I playing harder now?

Yes — 34% more effort per minute since January. Time above 80% of max HR rose from 5% to 39% of play. The trendline never reverted.

Am I playing more?

Less often than the Jul–Aug 2025 peak, but each session burns far more. May 2026 nearly matched Aug 2025's calories in eight fewer hours.

Is it making me healthier?

In-game fitness, unambiguously. Recovery markers, not yet — sleep is absorbing the gains. Section 08 has the verdict.

08

What the Body Says

The honest audit. The game got harder; recovery markers did not follow. Resting HR drifted from 58 to 66 bpm and HRV is flat. The data points at one suspect — and it isn't the sport.

Resting heart rate

RHR improved to 57.6 during the Jul–Aug 2025 volume peak, then drifted up through the layoff and has held mid-60s despite harder play. Volume helped it; intensity alone has not.

The verdict

The sessions are clean: next-day recovery is identical to rest days and resting HR is marginally better after playing. The leak is sleep — the 8–11 pm games cost ~30 minutes each against a 5.5 h baseline with no buffer. The 2026 intensity is earned fitness, but it will only surface in HRV and RHR once the nights stop paying for the evenings. Jul–Aug 2025 proves the upside: the one period where volume, RHR, HRV and VO₂max all moved together. Recommendation: same gear, earlier games, in bed by 11:30.
